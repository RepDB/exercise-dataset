90/90 Switch

A hip-mobility drill in which you sit with both knees bent at right angles and rotate your legs from one side to the other, keeping the 90/90 position on each side. It trains the internal and external rotation of both hips as you switch.

Upper legs · Bodyweight · Intermediate

Muscles worked by the 90/90 Switch

Primary

Hip FlexorsHip Flexor Muscles

Secondary

Also called: hip flexor, iliopsoas, psoas, iliacus, hip flexors.

Equipment

No equipment — this is a bodyweight exercise.

How to do the 90/90 Switch

  1. Sit on the floor with the front knee bent at 90 degrees and the back knee bent at 90 degrees, and place your hands behind you for support.
  2. Sit tall and keep your trunk upright.
  3. Lift both knees slightly and rotate your legs to the other side in a controlled movement, so that the front leg becomes the back leg.
  4. Settle into the 90/90 position on the new side, using your hands for balance.
  5. Switch back to the first side, and repeat for the planned repetitions.
  6. Keep the movement slow and smooth, without forcing the hips.

Form tips

  • Keep your trunk tall; leaning back too far takes the work out of the hips.
  • Do the 90/90 hold first if you cannot reach the position on both sides.
  • Move slowly and stop if you feel pinching in the hip or pain in the knee.
