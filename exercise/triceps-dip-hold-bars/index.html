Triceps Dip Hold on Dip Bars

An isometric hold at the bottom of the parallel-bar dip: you lower yourself between the bars and stop with the elbows bent, leaning slightly forward. The triceps, the chest and the front of the shoulders stay under tension at the point where the dip is hardest.

How to do the Triceps Dip Hold on Dip Bars

  1. Grip the parallel bars of a dip station, press up and straighten your arms with your shoulders pushed down away from your ears.
  2. Lean slightly forward, keep your legs together and cross your feet behind you if you want.
  3. Lower yourself slowly by bending your elbows until your upper arms are about parallel to the floor.
  4. Stop there and hold, with your elbows pointing back and your chest open.
  5. Keep your shoulders down and your body steady while you hold for the planned time.
  6. Press back up to straight arms and step down.

Form tips

  • Do not sink so far that your shoulders roll forward; if they do, hold a little higher.
  • Keep the elbows close to your body to load the triceps; flaring them sends the work to the chest and the shoulders.
  • Use a bench or a pair of stable boxes under your feet if the hold is too hard at first.
