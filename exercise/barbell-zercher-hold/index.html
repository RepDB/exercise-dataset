Barbell Zercher Hold

A standing hold with a barbell cradled in the crooks of your elbows against your abdomen. The abs and the trunk stabilizers resist the pull of the bar, while the biceps and the upper back keep it close to your body.

How to do the Barbell Zercher Hold

  1. Set a barbell on a rack at about the height of your lower ribs and place the crooks of your elbows under the bar.
  2. Fold your arms and clasp your hands together in front of your chest to lock the bar against your body.
  3. Lift the bar off the rack and step back, standing with your feet about shoulder-width apart.
  4. Stand tall, brace your abs and keep your ribs down, so that the bar rests against your abdomen without pulling you forward.
  5. Hold the position for the planned time, breathing slowly and keeping your elbows pressed together.
  6. Return the bar to the rack, or lower it to the floor with control.

Form tips

  • Wrap a towel or a barbell pad around the bar, since it presses into the elbows and the abdomen.
  • Keep the bar close to your body and do not lean back to carry it; if you do, the weight is too heavy.
  • Start with a bar a little lighter than your Zercher squat load, since the bar rests on your arms for the whole hold.
