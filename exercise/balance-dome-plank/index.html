Balance Dome Plank

A forearm plank with the elbows on a balance dome. The unstable surface makes the shoulders and the whole trunk work harder to keep the body in a straight line from head to heels.

How to do the Balance Dome Plank

  1. Place the balance dome on a flat floor with the domed side up and kneel behind it.
  2. Put your forearms on the dome with your elbows under your shoulders.
  3. Step your feet back into a plank with your body in a straight line from head to heels.
  4. Squeeze your glutes and brace your abdominals, keeping your neck long.
  5. Hold the position for the planned time while the dome moves under you.
  6. Lower your knees to the floor to finish.

Form tips

  • Do not let your hips sag or pike up; a straight line from head to heels is the target.
  • Press your forearms into the dome and keep your shoulders away from your ears.
  • Make it easier by widening your feet, or harder by bringing them closer together.
