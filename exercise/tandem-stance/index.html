Tandem Stance

A balance hold in which one foot is placed directly in front of the other, heel touching toes, so your base of support is as narrow as a line. Holding the position with your arms out trains the ankles and hips to keep the body steady.

Lower legs · Bodyweight · Beginner

Equipment

No equipment — this is a bodyweight exercise.

How to do the Tandem Stance

  1. Stand tall with your feet together and your arms out to the sides at shoulder height.
  2. Step one foot forward and place it directly in front of the other, heel against toes.
  3. Spread your weight evenly over both feet and keep your knees soft.
  4. Look at a fixed point ahead of you and keep your hips and shoulders level.
  5. Hold the position for the planned time, using your arms to correct small sways.
  6. Swap the front foot and repeat.

Form tips

  • Do this beside a counter or wall at first so you can reach out if you lose your balance.
  • Keep your gaze on a fixed point; looking down makes it harder.
  • Make it harder by closing your eyes or standing on a folded towel once you can hold it for 30 seconds.
