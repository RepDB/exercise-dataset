World's Greatest Stretch

A flowing mobility drill that combines a deep lunge with a trunk rotation. From a low lunge with both hands on the floor, you turn your chest toward the front leg and reach one arm to the ceiling, opening the hips, the upper back and the shoulder in one movement.

Full body · Bodyweight · Beginner

Equipment

No equipment — this is a bodyweight exercise.

How to do the World's Greatest Stretch

  1. Start in a push-up position with your hands under your shoulders and your body in a straight line.
  2. Step one foot forward, outside the same-side hand, into a deep lunge and keep the back leg straight.
  3. Keep the hand on the back-leg side planted under your shoulder and sink your hips toward the floor.
  4. Rotate your chest toward the front leg, lifting the arm on that side up until it points at the ceiling, and follow your hand with your eyes.
  5. Hold the open position for a breath, then lower your hand back to the floor.
  6. Step back to the push-up position and repeat on the other side, alternating sides for the planned repetitions.

Form tips

  • Keep the back knee off the floor and the back leg active so the stretch runs through the front of the hip.
  • Rotate from the upper back rather than leaning sideways; the hips stay square and low throughout.
  • Move slowly and breathe out as you rotate; the stretch should feel open and controlled, never forced.
