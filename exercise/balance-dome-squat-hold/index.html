Balance Dome Squat Hold

A static squat held on a balance dome: you stand on the domed top and lower into a partial squat with your arms reaching forward, then stay there while the unstable surface keeps shifting. The quadriceps and glutes hold the position and the ankles and trunk work to keep you balanced.

How to do the Balance Dome Squat Hold

  1. Place the balance dome on a flat floor with the domed side up and step onto the middle of it with both feet, about hip-width apart.
  2. Stand tall and find your balance before you start to squat.
  3. Push your hips back and bend your knees until your thighs are close to parallel with the floor.
  4. Reach your arms straight out in front of you to counterbalance and keep your chest up.
  5. Hold the position for the planned time, keeping your knees in line with your toes.
  6. Stand up slowly and step off one foot at a time.

Form tips

  • Master the plain balance dome stand first; the squat adds load to an unstable surface.
  • Spread your weight across the whole foot and do not let your heels lift or your knees cave inward.
  • Hold a wall or a rack with one hand while you learn the position.
