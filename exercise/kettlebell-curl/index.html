Kettlebell Curl

A biceps curl with one kettlebell held by the handle in both hands. The kettlebell hangs in front of your thighs and you curl it up toward your chest, keeping the elbows close to your sides; sharing the load between two hands makes it an easy way to start training the biceps.

How to do the Kettlebell Curl

  1. Stand with your feet about shoulder-width apart and hold the kettlebell by its handle with both hands, so it hangs in front of your thighs.
  2. Keep your elbows close to your sides, your chest tall and your shoulders relaxed.
  3. Bend your elbows and curl the kettlebell up toward your chest without letting your upper arms swing forward.
  4. Squeeze your biceps at the top, with the kettlebell close to your chest.
  5. Lower the kettlebell slowly until your arms are straight again, keeping control of the swing of the bell.
  6. Repeat for the planned repetitions.

Form tips

  • Hold the handle firmly with both hands and keep your wrists straight, so the kettlebell does not roll or tilt.
  • Do not lean back or swing your hips to lift the kettlebell; if you have to, the weight is too heavy.
  • Take about twice as long to lower the kettlebell as to lift it, so the biceps work through the whole descent.
