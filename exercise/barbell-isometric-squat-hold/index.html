Barbell Isometric Squat Hold

An isometric version of the barbell back squat: you squat down with the bar on your upper back and hold the bottom position. The quadriceps, glutes and the muscles that keep your back rigid stay under tension at the deepest, hardest point of the squat.

How to do the Barbell Isometric Squat Hold

  1. Set the bar on your upper back, grip it just outside your shoulders and step back from the rack with your feet about shoulder-width apart.
  2. Take a big breath into your belly, brace your core and keep your chest tall.
  3. Sit down and back until your thighs are about parallel to the floor or a little below, with your knees tracking over your toes.
  4. Stop at the bottom and hold the position, with your weight over the middle of your feet.
  5. Keep your back flat and your knees pushed out while you hold for the planned time.
  6. Drive up through your feet to stand, then rack the bar.

Form tips

  • Use safety arms or pins set just below your bottom position, since a long hold at depth is tiring.
  • Choose a lighter bar than your working squat weight; holding for time is harder than it looks.
  • Keep the knees pushed out over the toes instead of letting them collapse inward as you tire.
