Barbell Box Squat

A back squat with a barbell in which you sit back onto a box or bench at the bottom, pause for a moment and stand up again. The box fixes the depth and teaches you to sit back into the hips, and the brief pause removes the rebound out of the bottom.

How to do the Barbell Box Squat

  1. Set a box or bench behind you at a height where your thighs end up about parallel to the floor when you sit on it.
  2. Take the barbell on your upper back, step back and stand with your feet a little wider than shoulder-width.
  3. Brace your trunk and push your hips back, then lower yourself under control toward the box.
  4. Sit onto the box without dropping onto it, keeping your chest up and your shins close to vertical.
  5. Pause briefly with your muscles still tight, without relaxing your back.
  6. Drive through your whole foot to stand up, and repeat for the planned repetitions.

Form tips

  • Sit back rather than down; the box should be touched with your hips, not rested on with your weight.
  • Keep the torso tight in the pause; relaxing on the box is where most back injuries happen.
  • Use a box that cannot slide, and lower it as you progress until you can squat to full depth.
