Banded Overhead Press

A standing overhead press with a resistance band under your feet. You hold the band at shoulder height and press your hands overhead until your arms are straight, against the growing tension of the band. It trains the shoulders and the triceps without weights.

How to do the Banded Overhead Press

  1. Stand on the middle of a resistance band with your feet about hip-width apart.
  2. Hold the ends of the band at shoulder height with your palms facing forward and your elbows bent.
  3. Brace your trunk and squeeze your glutes to keep your ribs down.
  4. Press your hands straight up overhead until your arms are fully extended.
  5. Pause briefly at the top, then lower your hands back to your shoulders under control.
  6. Complete the planned repetitions.

Form tips

  • Choose a band that lets you finish the set with a steady press and without arching your back.
  • Press in a straight line close to your face and finish with your arms next to your ears.
  • Wrap the ends of the band around your hands for more tension, or use a lighter band to make it easier.
