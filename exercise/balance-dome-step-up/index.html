Balance Dome Step-Up

A single-leg step-up onto a balance dome: you place one foot on the domed top, stand up on it and then step back down. The surface tilts under your foot, so the quadriceps and glutes lift the body while the hips and ankle work to keep you steady.

How to do the Balance Dome Step-Up

  1. Stand facing the balance dome, placed domed side up on a flat floor.
  2. Place one foot flat in the middle of the dome.
  3. Press through the whole foot and straighten that leg to stand up on the dome, bringing your other foot beside it.
  4. Stand tall for a moment and keep your weight centred over the dome.
  5. Step back down with the other foot first, then the working foot.
  6. Complete the planned repetitions on one leg, then switch legs.

Form tips

  • Put your foot in the middle of the dome; stepping near the edge makes it tip.
  • Push through the heel and the whole foot rather than hopping up from the back leg.
  • Hold a wall or a rack with one hand until you can step up and down smoothly.
