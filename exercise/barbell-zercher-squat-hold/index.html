Barbell Zercher Squat Hold

An isometric version of the Zercher squat: you squat down with the barbell in the crooks of your elbows and hold the bottom position. The quadriceps, glutes and the trunk stay under tension at the deepest point, while the arms and upper back keep the bar close to you.

How to do the Barbell Zercher Squat Hold

  1. Set a barbell on a rack at about the height of your lower ribs, place the crooks of your elbows under the bar and clasp your hands in front of your chest.
  2. Lift the bar off the rack, step back and stand with your feet about shoulder-width apart.
  3. Brace your abs and keep your chest tall, then sit down and back until your thighs are about parallel to the floor.
  4. Stop at the bottom and hold, with your elbows resting inside your knees and your knees pushed out over your toes.
  5. Keep the bar close to your abdomen and your back straight while you hold for the planned time.
  6. Drive up through your feet to stand and return the bar to the rack.

Form tips

  • Wrap a towel or a barbell pad around the bar, since it presses into the elbows and the abdomen.
  • Use safety pins set just below your bottom position, since a long hold at depth is tiring.
  • Keep the chest tall; if the bar pulls you forward and your back rounds, the weight is too heavy.
