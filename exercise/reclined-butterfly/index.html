Reclined Butterfly

A restful version of the butterfly stretch: lying back with the soles of your feet together and your knees dropped out to the sides, you open the inner thighs and hips under gravity. Resting your back and arms on the floor lets the legs relax into the stretch.

Upper legs · Bodyweight · Beginner

Muscles worked by the Reclined Butterfly

Also called: hip adductors, inner thigh, adductor muscles, groin.

Equipment

No equipment — this is a bodyweight exercise.

How to do the Reclined Butterfly

  1. Lie on your back with your knees bent and the soles of your feet flat on the floor.
  2. Bring the soles of your feet together and let both knees fall out to the sides.
  3. Rest your arms on the floor with your palms up, or place your hands on your belly.
  4. Keep your lower back resting on the floor and your shoulders relaxed.
  5. Breathe slowly and let your knees sink toward the floor for the planned time.
  6. Bring your knees together with your hands to finish.

Form tips

  • Do not push the knees down with your hands; let gravity do the work so the inner thighs relax.
  • Slide your feet further from the hips to ease the stretch, or closer to deepen it.
  • Put a pillow or folded blanket under each knee if the stretch feels too strong.
