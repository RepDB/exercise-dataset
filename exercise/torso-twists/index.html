Torso Twists

A standing rotational warm-up that turns the rib cage from side to side while the pelvis stays comparatively stable, mobilising the thoracic spine and trunk.

Core · Bodyweight · Beginner

Muscles worked by the Torso Twists

Primary

ObliquesOblique AbdominalsDeep CoreTransversus Abdominis

Secondary

Lower BackErector Spinae

Also called: oblique, obliques, side abs, external oblique, internal oblique, transverse abdominis.

Equipment

No equipment — this is a bodyweight exercise.

How to do the Torso Twists

  1. Stand with the feet about shoulder-width apart and the knees softly bent.
  2. Brace lightly and hold the hands together in front of the chest.
  3. Rotate the rib cage to one side without shifting the feet.
  4. Return through centre and rotate to the other side.
  5. Continue alternating smoothly through a comfortable range.

Form tips

  • Turn from the upper back rather than wrenching the lower spine.
  • Keep the hips mostly forward and distribute the weight evenly between the feet.
  • Use a controlled rhythm; this is mobility work, not a ballistic swing.
